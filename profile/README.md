## Welcome to Forestritium

### The Ecosystem for Developers
Welcome to Forestritium. This is a collaborative space designed for growth and experimentation. This organization serves as a collective workshop where members can learn, review, and evolve together through interconnectedness and shared resources.

### Our Mission
Forestritium is a volunteer-driven ecosystem where members:
* Volunteer their precious time to review and collab with each other.
* Volunteer on innovative projects.
* Share their work and knowledge to bridge the gap between basic functionality and technical excellence.

# Forestritium Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

To make this community a haven for developers, we as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to making participation in our project and our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of their age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or any other personal factors.

## Operational Protocol

### Onboarding

- All new members must post an introduction within 48 hours of joining.
- A role will be assigned based on your introduction, unlocking the rest of the production floor.
- Need more time? DM any Maintainer or Administrator within those 48 hours for a 7-day grace period.
- Unregistered accounts will be pruned. No lurkers allowed.

## Community Standards

### Be Respectful

Treat every person with the dignity you would want extended to yourself. Harassment, personal attacks, and exclusionary behavior will not be tolerated on any platform or in any context.

### Healthy Debate

Disagree freely with ideas. Keep critiques focused on logic and reasoning, not on the person behind them.

### Constructive Feedback

When reviewing work, focus on helping the other person improve. Explain your reasoning. A review that teaches is more valuable than one that merely corrects.

### Give Credit

Cite your sources. Respect open-source licenses and attribution requirements. Acknowledge what you have built upon.

## Prohibited Conduct

### Integrity First

No NSFW content, harassment, doxxing, or malicious scripts. We build; we do not break.

### No Unsolicited DMs/Spam

Do not DM members for general tasks, to pitch products, recruit without permission, or beg for free tech support.

### Report Violations

Report violations immediately to a maintainer or moderator.

**Contact:** admin.forestritium@gmail.com

## Maintenance and Inactivity Policy

To keep the Forestritium ecosystem active and useful, we follow a 1-year activity rule.

### Projects

If a repository has received no updates for over 1 year, the owner must either:

- Delete the repository, or
- Add one of the following files to the root:
  - `COMPLETED.md` — only if the project is fully mature and will not require any updates for a while.
  - `DISCONTINUED.md`
  - `INACTIVE.md`

The file should state the reason for the pause and an expected restart date, if one is planned.

### Members

Members who are inactive for more than 6 months may be flagged or removed from the active member list to ensure it accurately reflects who is currently contributing.

## Wellbeing

### Time Away

We value "AFK" time.

If you need a break for life, family, or personal reasons, take it. Forestritium will be here when you get back.

## Enforcement

Maintainers and Administrators have final say on rule interpretations.

Violations of this Code of Conduct are taken seriously. Enforcement actions may include a formal warning, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from the organization, depending on the nature and severity of the violation.

If you see something that breaks the ecosystem, report it to the maintainers. All reports will be handled with discretion and care.

## Contact the Maintainers

**Email:** admin.forestritium@gmail.com

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially or unofficially representing the community in public or other spaces.
